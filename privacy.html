FLUX Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

The short version

  • FLUX works without an account. Without one, nothing you put in FLUX leaves your device, unless you choose to sync with your own devices over Wi-Fi or save a backup file.
  • If you create an account, FLUX stores only what friends and chat need: your email, username, name, picture, friends and messages.
  • Cloud sync is optional. If you turn it on, your FLUX data is kept in your account so you can use it on any device.
  • No ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold.

Who runs FLUX

FLUX is an independent app made in Malta by a solo developer. Under data protection law, FLUX's developer is the "controller" of account data. Contact: fluxlifeos@gmail.com

What stays on your device

Your quests, habits, goals, notes, journal, check-ins, focus sessions, fitness, budget, calendar, themes and settings are stored only on your device, unless you turn on cloud sync.

  • Wi-Fi sync goes directly between your own devices, encrypted, and never through FLUX's server.
  • Backups are files that you save and control.
  • Calendar import: if you import events from your phone's calendar or a file, they are copied into FLUX on your device only. FLUX only reads your calendar, never changes it.

Cloud sync (optional)

If you turn on cloud sync, FLUX keeps a copy of your FLUX data in your account so you can use it on any device: quests, habits, goals, notes, journal, check-ins, focus sessions, fitness, budget, calendar, settings, themes and skin pictures.

  • Your choice: it stays off until you turn it on and tick that you agree. Because check-ins and journal entries can say things about your health and feelings, FLUX asks for your clear consent first.
  • Who can see it: it is locked to your account, so no other FLUX user can see it. It is not end-to-end encrypted, so the person running FLUX could technically access it, which only happens if the law requires it.
  • Where: Supabase keeps it on servers in the European Union.
  • Stopping: turn it off any time in Settings, Cloud sync, and choose whether to delete the cloud copy. Deleting your account deletes it too. The copy on your devices always stays.

Update checks

The Android app checks FLUX's server about twice a day, and when you open it, to see if a newer version is out. This sends nothing about you. Like any connection, the hosting provider briefly keeps technical logs, such as the IP address, for security. You can switch update notifications off in Settings, Notifications.

What FLUX stores if you create an account

  • Account: your email address and password. The password is stored scrambled (hashed), so no one can read it.
  • Profile: your username, name, picture (an emoji, or a photo you upload, which FLUX shrinks to a small square on your device first), banner (colours or a picture you upload), status, bio, and any badges the FLUX team has given you (like Beta tester). Unless you switch it off, it also shows your FLUX stats: level, streak, trophy count, trophies you choose to show off, and your quests and focus time this week. Your friends, and people you send a friend request to, can see your profile.
  • Friends: your friend requests, friends and the people you block.
  • Messages: what you send and receive, when it was sent and whether it was read.
  • Reports: if you report someone, your report and the message you reported.
  • Notification key: a random code that lets your phone check for new messages in the background.
  • Security logs: the hosting provider keeps short-term technical logs, like IP addresses, to keep the service secure.

FLUX does not store your birthday. It asks for your age when you sign up only to check that you are 13 or older.

Why FLUX uses this data

  • To run your account, friends and chat, which is the service you asked for.
  • To keep people safe: handling reports and stopping spam and abuse. This is a legitimate interest.
  • To follow the law when it requires something.

Who can see it

  • Your friends see your profile and the messages you send them.
  • Messages are protected by access rules on every table, so in the app only you and the person you are talking to can read a chat.
  • Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. They are stored on FLUX's server, so the person running FLUX could technically access them. FLUX's maker only looks at messages when handling a report, or when the law requires it.
  • Supabase hosts FLUX's server and stores account data on servers in the European Union.
  • The email provider sends your sign-up and password codes.
  • Your data is never sold or used for advertising.

How long data is kept

  • Account data is kept until you delete your account. Deleting it permanently removes your profile, friends list and every message you sent or received.
  • Single messages: you can unsend your own messages at any time.
  • Reports are kept for up to 12 months for safety, then deleted.
  • Backup copies at the hosting provider can take a short time to clear after something is deleted.

Your rights

Under data protection law (GDPR) you can:

  • See and take your data: Settings, Account, Download my data.
  • Fix it: edit your profile at any time.
  • Delete it: Settings, Account, Delete account.
  • Object, ask questions or make any other request: email fluxlifeos@gmail.com.

You can also complain to Malta's data protection authority, the Information and Data Protection Commissioner (idpc.org.mt).

Children

You must be at least 13 to create an account. Some countries require a parent's permission below a higher age, 14, 15 or 16 in some EU countries. If that applies to you, you need your parent's or guardian's permission before signing up. If FLUX learns that an account belongs to someone under 13, it will be deleted. Parents can contact fluxlifeos@gmail.com.

Security

  • In transit: all connections use encryption (HTTPS).
  • Access rules: every table is locked so people can only reach their own data and their friends' chats.
  • Passwords: they are hashed, so no one can read them.
  • Anti-spam: there are limits on how fast messages and friend requests can be sent.

No system is perfect, so use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. For important changes, FLUX will tell you in the app.
